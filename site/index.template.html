What it does

  • Live heart rate from a Bluetooth chest strap, a watch that broadcasts heart rate, or a Galaxy Fit3.
  • Five heart-rate zones (from your age and resting heart rate) with different vibrations for above and below your zone.
  • GPS runs with pace, splits, laps, map and spoken cues; walks, treadmill, cycling and strength too.
  • Routes: draw your own on the map, let Taal plan a loop of any distance from where you stand, or import a GPX, then follow it with off-route alerts.
  • After each activity: title, note, effort and achievements (fastest 1 km, 5 km, longest run…), plus an image to share on Instagram or WhatsApp.
  • Structured workouts, a training calendar, load and recovery, VO2max and race predictions.
  • Optional upload to Strava and Samsung Health (Health Connect). No account, no ads, no tracking: your data stays on your phone.

Install

  1. Tap Download above. If Chrome asks, choose Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android asks to allow installs from your browser: Settings → Allow from this source, then go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect asks, choose More details → Install anyway (the app isn't on the Play Store yet).
  4. Open Taal and follow the setup: permissions, your profile, your sensor, and background running.

Updates: the app tells you when a new version is out. Download it the same way and tap Update; your data stays.

Before every run
  • Turn Power saving / Battery saver off: on many phones it switches GPS off with the screen off.
  • Do the background steps for your phone once (Taal shows them in setup and under Device → Background running). Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO, Oppo, Realme, OnePlus, Vivo and iQOO phones need them, or the run stops in your pocket.

Your first week as a tester

  1. An easy run with a zone target (Run → Zone 2): did the vibrations come at the right times?
  2. A run with the phone in your pocket and the screen off the whole time: is the distance right compared with a watch or Strava?
  3. Connect Strava or Samsung Health (Device tab) and check the run arrives.

After each run, or whenever something looks wrong: Device → Feedback. It opens your email or chat app with your phone details and the app log attached. You see everything before you send it.

Good to know

  • Taal is not a medical device. Zones and training numbers are estimates for fitness use.
  • Taal is an independent app, not made by or affiliated with Samsung, Garmin or Strava. Galaxy Fit3 band data is an unofficial, experimental option, off by default.
  • Uninstalling deletes your activities. Use Device → Your data → Save backup file first.
